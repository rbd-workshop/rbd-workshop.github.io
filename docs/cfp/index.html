Call for papers

We invite archival and nonarchival papers, position papers, benchmarks, datasets, reproducibility studies, demonstrations, and diagnostic or negative results on capability retrieval for language agents.

See important dates on the homepage for submission deadlines and workshop timing.

Topics of interest

  • Representation and indexing. Language, schemas, code, demonstrations, traces, graphs, ontologies, embeddings, and dynamic indexes for tools, APIs, skills, workflows, memories, models, and agents.
  • Retrieval, routing, and composition. Selection of tools and APIs, skills, workflows, memories, models, and agents; joint retrieval; and selection that accounts for dependencies, compatibility, permissions, reliability, and cost.
  • Retrieval in the agent loop. Retrieval conditioned on state, plans, and observations; reformulation, clarification, decomposition, incorporation, invocation, delegation, replanning, recovery, and abstention.
  • Action grounded evaluation. Evaluation of retrieval, incorporation, invocation, composition, and execution; metrics for complete sets and dependencies; calibration and resource constraints.
  • Ecosystems and deployment. Dynamic, federated, and access controlled registries; Model Context Protocol ecosystems; provenance, permissions, freshness, observability, reliability, caching, and recovery.

Retrieval augmented generation, memory, tool use, deep research, and multiagent work are in scope when capability retrieval, routing, or composition is central. Work assuming a fixed manually selected capability set is outside this scope.

Submission guidelines

Paper preparation

Page limits, formatting requirements, and supplementary material instructions will be announced.

Submission and review

  • Direct submissions: submissions are planned through OpenReview, subject to venue policy. The review process will be double blind, with three independent reviews per direct submission.
  • ARR commitments: commitments from ACL Rolling Review will follow the venue’s policy. Eligible cycles and commitment instructions will be announced.

The submission portal will be announced.

Publication options

We welcome archival and nonarchival submissions. Publication arrangements and eligibility details will be announced.
