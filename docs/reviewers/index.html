Program Committee Signup

We invite you to join the program committee for Retrieval Beyond Documents (RBD), an accepted workshop at NAACL 2027 in San Francisco. We welcome researchers from academia and industry, including graduate researchers with relevant experience.

We are looking for expertise in information retrieval, natural language processing, and language agents, particularly retrieval of tools, skills, workflows, and memory. See the call for papers for the full workshop scope.

Join the program committee

Open the Program Committee Signup form (Google Forms).

Please share your first and last names, email, affiliation, current role, areas of expertise, OpenReview ID, and how many papers you can review. Google Scholar or other research profile links are optional.

The organizers will be in touch about assignments and timing. You can update your details using the edit response link after submitting. Thank you for helping build the RBD research community.
