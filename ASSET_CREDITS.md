## Biography provenance

Organizer biographies use Section 5 of the user’s `Retrieval_beyond_document.pdf`, except Hitesh’s biography, which was rewritten from his current website at his request. PDF line breaks and ligatures are normalized, and prose hyphenation is simplified at the user’s request.

Hitesh’s biography was checked October 26, 2026 against his [homepage](https://hiteshlpatel.github.io/), [publications](https://hiteshlpatel.github.io/publications/), [projects](https://hiteshlpatel.github.io/projects/), [service](https://hiteshlpatel.github.io/service/), and [awards](https://hiteshlpatel.github.io/awards/). The homepage confirms **Principal Applied Scientist and Tech Lead** and his interests in reliable agents, reasoning, knowledge use, multilingual and multimodal models, and evaluation. The service page documents GRAIL-V organization, Area Chair roles, and open research contributions; the awards page documents the EMNLP 2025 Best Social Impact Paper Award. Background research sources include [RECOR](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.129/) for reasoning and conversational retrieval, [Hybrid AI for Responsive Multi-Turn Online Conversations](https://aclanthology.org/2025.knowledgenlp-1.20.pdf) for knowledge use and adaptive dialogue systems, and [Aligning LLMs for Multilingual Consistency](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-industry.9/) for training and multilingual reliability. These inform a broad research biography without paper titles or performance claims in the public text. The current homepage and the user’s explicit title correction take precedence over the older title still shown on some subpages.
